# MediFind
MediFind is a website that helps users find medicines, compare pharmacy prices, check availability and locate nearby pharmacies using their city or area. 

System Actors
1. Customer
A customer is a person who uses MediFind to search for medicines and find information about pharmacies, medicine prices, and availability.

Responsibilities:Search for medicines by name.View medicine information.Compare medicine prices from different pharmacies.Check medicine availability.Search for pharmacies by city or area.View pharmacy details and contact information.

2. Pharmacy
A pharmacy represents a pharmacy business or an authorized person responsible for managing its information on MediFind.

Responsibilities:Add medicines to the pharmacy's listings.Update medicine prices.Update medicine availability.Manage pharmacy information.Maintain medicine stock information.

3. Administrator
The administrator is responsible for overseeing and maintaining the MediFind platform to ensure that its information is properly organized.

Responsibilities:Manage customer and pharmacy information.Manage pharmacy records.Manage medicine information.Review and maintain platform data.Monitor the overall system.
